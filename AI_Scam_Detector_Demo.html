Safety checklist

  • Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, or remote-access codes.
  • Do not trust guaranteed or unusually high returns.
  • Verify a firm or intermediary through official regulator sources independently.
  • Do not send money because someone creates urgency.

This prototype is educational, not a definitive fraud verdict. A low warning level does not prove that a message is safe.
